# Top Partner Ecosystem Platform Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Partner Relationship Management (PRM), Partner Portals, Channel Management, Ecosystem Mapping, Co-Selling, Partner Enablement & Partner Revenue*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Partner Ecosystem Management**. These tools help organizations recruit, onboard, enable, manage, and activate partners while supporting partner portals, deal registration, account mapping, referrals, co-selling, partner marketing, commissions, certifications, partner analytics, and ecosystem-driven revenue.

**Examples** include Crossbeam, Reveal, PartnerStack, Impartner, Allbound, Zift Solutions, Kiflo, Channeltivity, Ecosystem, and PartnerFleet. Modern platforms increasingly connect partner relationships with CRM accounts, pipeline, co-selling, attribution, and revenue rather than functioning solely as traditional partner portals. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source partner-management, referral-management, CRM, portal, marketplace, affiliate, and ecosystem-building projects. There are significantly fewer mature open-source products dedicated specifically to enterprise PRM than proprietary platforms, so the open-source section also includes adjacent projects that can be combined to build a self-hosted partner ecosystem platform.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or repositories.

- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)** for lightweight self-hosted CRM and relationship management.
- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)** for modern open-source CRM infrastructure.
- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)** for CRM + sales + portal + commerce workflows.
- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** for integrated CRM, sales, accounting, and business operations.
- **[Keycloak](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)** for partner portal identity, SSO, RBAC, and organization management.
- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)** for partner performance analytics.
- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)** for self-hosted ecosystem dashboards.
- **[PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)** for measuring partner portal and ecosystem engagement.

**Frameworks for building custom partner ecosystem systems**: Combine **PartnerHub / SuiteCRM / Twenty / Odoo** with **Keycloak**, **PostgreSQL**, **Redis**, **Metabase / Apache Superset**, and a custom API layer using **Django / FastAPI / Node.js**.

A typical open-source architecture can therefore look like:

**CRM / ERP → Partner Management → Partner Portal → Account Mapping → Deal Registration → Co-Selling → Commissions → Analytics**

For a more sophisticated ecosystem, additional services can provide:

**Partner Directory + Partner Matching + Referral Tracking + Deal Registration + Training/Certification + MDF + Incentives + Revenue Attribution + Partner Analytics**

The open-source ecosystem is currently much stronger for the **building blocks of partner management** than for complete enterprise PRM. GitHub's partner-management topic, for example, contains dedicated partner-management and referral projects such as PartnerHub, Ally, Partner-Flow-FE, and Impact. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

This makes the category particularly suitable for a **composable PRM architecture**, where an organization combines an open-source CRM, identity layer, partner portal, workflow engine, referral/commission service, and BI platform instead of adopting one monolithic proprietary PRM.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Partner ecosystem platforms vary considerably in their focus: PRM, partner portals, account mapping, affiliate management, channel marketing, co-selling, or partner revenue.
- Open-source projects listed here may provide only part of the functionality of enterprise PRM platforms and may require significant customization.
- Always verify current licensing terms, project activity, security posture, and production readiness before deploying an open-source component.
- Partner and customer relationship data can contain commercially sensitive information; appropriate security, privacy, access-control, and data-governance practices should be implemented.
